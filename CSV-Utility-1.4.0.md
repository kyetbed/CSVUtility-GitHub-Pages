# CSV Utility 1.4.0

This release adds familiar spreadsheet drag interactions and a focused
side-by-side workspace for working across two CSV files at once.

- Moves a selected cell or multi-cell range by dragging its outer border, with
  a hand cursor, live destination outline, preserved formatting, and undo.
- Adds a round fill handle at the selection corner for quickly repeating cell
  values and styles across a larger range.
- Opens a second sheet beside the current one from the tab's themed
  **Show in Split Screen** context-menu command, with an adjustable divider and
  a maximum of two visible sheets.
- Keeps the complete Find, Sort, Diff, Filter, and Functions tool strip visible
  in both split panes and routes each action to the pane that invoked it.
- Keeps split-pane toolbars aligned when a full-width sheet is resized and
  clearly marks both visible tabs while preserving the active pane state.
